ENGLISH SUMMARY

Privacy is the product vision — not yet a production guarantee.

KonaklamaFlow is exploring a Proton-inspired, independent client-held-key, zero-access architecture for future hotel guest records. Its current browser Web Crypto lab uses synthetic data only. The service is not a deployed encrypted hotel PMS, is not certified, and has not undergone independent security validation. Future hotel security claims would require audited architecture, working role-key lifecycle, lawful reporting integrations and real pilot evidence. This project is not affiliated with Proton.
